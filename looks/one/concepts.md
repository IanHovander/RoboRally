# Robo Rally: 50 looks (designer one)

Each one covers the world, camera, light, palette, type, motion, sound and how the phone feels. The lane for this
copy is **cinematic realism**. The pick is at the bottom.

1. **Last Light.** An old locomotive works in the last hour of sun: tall steel windows, shafts of light in the dust,
   polished concrete, steel deck plates. Each round the sun drops lower, until the hall lamps come on for the finish.
   The frame is letterboxed, the camera behaves like a film crew, and the phone is a graphite storyboard.
2. **Night Shift.** A factory at 3 a.m. under sodium-vapour lamps, with wet concrete, orange pools of light and deep
   blue shadow. Haze, anamorphic streaks on the lamps, a slow handheld drift. On the phone, cards glow like dashboard dials.
3. **Salt Flat Trials.** Robots racing on a dry lake bed at golden hour, with the course chalked onto the salt, heat
   shimmer and mile-long shadows. Wide lens, dust plumes, and a pennant flag at each checkpoint.
4. **Hangar in the Rain.** A huge aircraft hangar with its doors open on a storm. Floodlights, rain sheeting past the
   door, puddles reflecting the lasers. The sound is rain on a steel roof.
5. **Tabletop Macro.** Painted miniatures on a scarred oak workbench, shot with a macro lens, so the depth of field
   is a few millimetres. A warm desk lamp, sawdust, the shadow of a giant coffee mug. The phone is a hobby tray of cards.
6. **Clean Room.** A white semiconductor fab under amber safelight. The robots are sealed white units, the light is
   soft and has no shadows, and the camera moves like a slow surgical robot. Quiet, clinical, eerie.
7. **Lunar Test Grid.** A proving ground on the Moon: one hard sun, a black sky, Earth on the horizon, and fine dust
   that kicks up and falls without air. No sound except servos heard through the robots' own frames.
8. **Parking Structure, Tokyo.** Level B3 of a concrete garage at midnight: fluorescent strips, painted bay numbers,
   reflections in oil stains, neon bleeding in from the street. Slow pans like a 90s crime film.
9. **Watchmaker's Bench.** Brass automata racing across a watch movement: jewels, springs, a loupe's eye view,
   warm tungsten light. Gears are real escapement wheels. The phone feels like engraved brass.
10. **Container Port at Dusk.** Stacks of rusted containers, gantry cranes against a purple sky, and a course painted
    on the dock apron. A tug horn sounds in the distance at each checkpoint.
11. **Greenhouse.** A Victorian glasshouse at noon: dappled light through leaves, condensation, terracotta, robots
    weaving between planters. Soft, green and gold. Birdsong.
12. **Cathedral of Machines.** A gothic nave converted into a robot foundry: stained-glass light, candles, stone. The
    camera moves on slow, reverent cranes, and a choir pad swells at each flag.
13. **Broadcast Sport.** The race as live TV: a five-camera production, a lower-third package, a replay wipe on
    every crash and a commentary-style sting. The phone is a team radio.
14. **Formula Telemetry.** The look of an F1 pit wall: dark glass, sector colours, live gap timings, a car's-eye
    onboard camera. Precise and technical.
15. **Ice Arena.** A hockey rink after hours: the course etched into the ice under the arena lights, and robots
    that slide a little. Cold white and blue, and every sound echoes.
16. **Film Noir.** Black and white with venetian-blind shadows, cigarette smoke and a single desk lamp. The lasers
    are the only colour.
17. **Desert Night Drive-In.** An outdoor course under the stars, lit by car headlights and a projector beam. Crickets
    and an old radio.
18. **Snow Globe.** The course is sealed in a glass dome on a mantelpiece. Every fall shakes the globe and sends
    snow everywhere. Cosy, warm room light.
19. **Polar Research Station.** Blue hour in Antarctica: orange-clad robots, sodium floodlights, blowing spindrift,
    the hum of a generator. The checkpoints are survey stakes.
20. **Rooftop Skyline.** A course on a skyscraper roof at dusk, with water towers, the city glittering below and
    helicopter wash. Falling off the edge is real vertigo.
21. **Nature Documentary.** The robots are treated like animals, filmed through a long lens from a hide. Slow motion
    for every pounce, a nature-doc score, and the phone as a field notebook.
22. **Museum After Hours.** The robots are exhibits come to life in a marble gallery: vitrines, brass placards, motion-
    sensor lights clicking on as they pass.
23. **Brutalist Concrete.** Raw board-formed concrete, a single skylight and strict geometry. Grey, warm, monumental

24. **Wes Symmetry.** Perfect centred symmetry, a pastel candy palette, whip-pans and dollhouse cutaways. The phone

30. **Bauhaus Toys.** Primary-coloured wooden blocks, circles and triangles on a beech table, and a 1920s poster

31. **Swiss Railway.** The clarity of a station clock: white, black and red, precise motion, and a sweeping second

37. **Pixel Isometric.** A hand-pixelled isometric factory with chunky sprites and chiptune. Crisp and readable.
38. **Holotable War Room.** The course as a hologram above a dark command table, with officers' silhouettes around
    it. Blue light, scan lines and a radar ping on each register.
39. **Bioluminescent Cave.** Robots crossing a cavern floor lit only by glowing fungi and their own headlamps. Deep
    teal, with dripping water.
40. **Deep Sea Rig.** An undersea habitat floor: caustics, drifting particles, and robots in diving-bell housings.
    Sonar pings and muffled clanks.
41. **Soviet Space Program.** Retro-futurist cosmonaut robots, enamel control panels, a stencilled Cyrillic
    typeface and a vacuum-tube hum.
42. **Studio Sound Stage.** The race as a film set seen from behind the camera: C-stands, softboxes, tape marks, a
    clapperboard per register. Meta and warm.
43. **Toy Store Window.** A wind-up tin robot race behind frosted shop glass at Christmas: tinsel, warm bulbs,
    clockwork ratchets.
44. **Chess Hall.** A grand tournament hall with wooden floor and green-shaded lamps, the course a giant chessboard
    in marble, and spectators murmuring.
45. **Monochrome Architectural Model.** A white foam-core model of a factory under one raking light, with robots as
    small resin figures. Pure form, long shadows, total calm.
46. **Mars Rover Field Trial.** Red dust, a pale butterscotch sky, robots like rovers. Telemetry stills arrive with a

47. **Rail Yard Turntable.** A roundhouse with a working locomotive turntable as the giant central gear, steam, oil

48. **Bowling Alley 1962.** Waxed maple lanes, neon script, pin-setter machinery as board elements, and the sound of

49. **Data-Centre Cold Aisle.** Racks of blinking servers, blue LED wash, the roar of fans, and robots as maintenance

50. **Garden at Dawn.** Robots the size of beetles crossing a dew-wet garden path among moss, pebbles and fallen

## The pick: **1. Last Light**

**Why this one.** It's cinematic realism through light, and light is the hardest thing to fake: it makes a frame
look *shot* rather than rendered. Raking sun through tall windows, haze you can see the beams in, real steel and
concrete. Nothing on screen tries to look slick; it just looks like a well-lit place.

- **A reason to look up.** The race has a time-of-day arc. Each round the sun sinks lower and redder, and on the
  final flag the hall lamps clank on and a spotlight finds the winner. The room is watching one film together.
- **The letterbox is the HUD.** A 2.39:1 frame, with all the information in the black bars (register count, clock,
  the cast with their cards), so nothing sits on the picture.
- **The phone is a storyboard.** You deal five cards into five film frames. During the race the phone goes dark,
  apart from the frame that's playing, so everyone looks up.
- **It teaches by showing.** The lobby runs a short trailer on a little test set: five frames fill, a robot plays
  them out, a belt carries it, a laser burns it, another robot is shoved into a pit and comes back, and flags 1 and
  2 fall. No words.

## What was built (look one)

- **Big screen:** `public/host.html`, `public/js/host.js` and `public/css/film.css` (the letterbox and the lobby).
  `games/robo-rally/lobby.js` and `lobby.css` hold the trailer, and `host.js` holds the race HUD.
  `scene.js` is the hall: sun, shafts, lens and camera director. `bots3d.js` has the robot materials, and
  `audio.js` synthesizes all the sound.
- **Phone:** `public/index.html` and `public/js/play.js` handle joining and your robot. `games/robo-rally/player.js` is
  the storyboard.
- **Shared:** `games/robo-rally/look.js` has the paint palette, robot models and portraits. `img/bot-N.webp` and
  `img/bot-N-mask.webp` are portraits rendered from the 3D models (`public/tools/portraits.html` re-renders them).
- **Font:** Inter (OFL), in `public/fonts/inter.woff2`.
- **Rules:** `engine.js`, `board.js`, `cards.js` and `rules.js` are unchanged.
- **Test URLs:** `host.html?fixed` keeps full quality on a slow machine (for screenshots), and `?flat` forces the
  no-WebGL fallback.
