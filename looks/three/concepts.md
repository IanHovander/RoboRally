# Robo Rally — fifty looks (designer three)

Each is a whole experience: the world on the big screen, its camera and light, the palette and type, how things
move and sound, and how the phone feels in your hand.

1. **Lumen** — a smoked-glass light table floating in a dark room. Every board element is drawn with light:
   belts are slow streams of light under the glass, pits are where the light is missing, walls are bright edges.
   Robots are glowing glass pebbles that leave long-exposure light trails. Thin type, glass-chime sound, phone cards like lit slides. **← chosen**
2. **Porcelain Studio** — an infinity-cove photo studio; matte white ceramic board and robots shot like products,
   soft area light and contact shadows, pastel player colors, marimba taps.

## The pick: Lumen

My lane is light, minimal, abstract. Lumen is the strongest there because **the light itself carries the rules**:
a belt is a visible flow of light, a pit is literally an absence of light, a laser is a line of light, and a robot's
path stays drawn on the glass for a moment after it moves, so anyone glancing up sees what just happened.
That makes the board readable from across a room with almost no words.

It stays slick without trying: one material (smoked glass), one light color (warm white) plus the players' own
colors, thin type, slow camera, soft glass sounds. No neon grid, no chrome, no gimmicks. The phone repeats the
idea in miniature: your cards are lit slides you drop into five slots, and a tiny light table shows the path
your program will draw before you commit.
