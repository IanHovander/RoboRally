# Checkpoint: 50 animation ideas for Lumen

Every idea plays Maya's Move 2 from (2,1) to (4,1) and ends inside the 3 s budget with the "after" state: Maya resting on flag 1 at (4,1) facing east, her red taken ring at the foot of column 1, Theo still at (5,3) facing west, both columns back to warm white, and the camera home.

## Banned (too obvious)

- The column flashes in her colour and swells (today's baseline).
- Shockwave rings spreading across the glass.
- Confetti, sparks or motes bursting up.
- A victory spin or twirl on the spot.
- A happy hop or bounce.
- The camera pushing in on the robot.
- Camera shake or a full-screen flash.
- A "CHECKPOINT!" / "FLAG 1" banner or achievement pop-up.
- A literal flag running up a pole and waving.
- Fireworks.
- The robot glowing or pulsing brighter, or a progress bar filling.
- Slow motion on the arrival.

## The ideas

1. **The abacus.** As Maya settles on the flag, a bead of red light slides down the white column from out of frame, picks up speed, and clacks to rest at its foot as her taken ring. *Twist:* The column was an abacus rod all along. The game already stacks each claimer's ring at the foot, so every later claim on this flag clicks another bead onto the pile, a tally anyone can read.
2. **Maypole.** The fading red trail she just drew from (2,1) peels off the glass, swings up and winds round the column like a maypole ribbon, then slides down and cinches into her ring. *Twist:* The path that got her here becomes the receipt for it, and the trail that normally fades away is kept.
3. **Ink in the glass.** At the claim her colour drops into the smoked slab under the flag and billows inside the glass like ink in water, soft red clouds curling out a space or two and slowly clearing until only the ring is left. *Twist:* The slab turns out to have depth. For once something happens inside the glass, not on it, and it's quiet where everyone expects loud.
4. **Red glass paperweight.** Maya turns translucent for a moment, and the column's light passes through her body, throwing moving red caustics on the glass around her like sun through a paperweight. The caustics draw in to form the ring as she turns solid again. *Twist:* The robot becomes Lumen's own material for a beat: the flag shines through her, not on her.
5. **Breath on the window.** The column's warmth fogs the glass in a soft pale disc around her. Her pointer drags one tally stroke through the fog, like a finger on a misted window, and as the fog evaporates the stroke becomes her ring. *Twist:* An everyday property of glass (it fogs) turned into scorekeeping. At later flags the fog briefly shows her earlier strokes before she adds the next one, building towards a tally gate.
6. **Her shadow points the way.** The column's light gathers to a point at its top and swings round. Maya's shadow, a patch of real darkness on the lit glass, stretches across the board until its tip touches flag 2, then shrinks back as the column returns. *Twist:* Lumen draws everything with light, so the one hint is drawn with its absence: her own shadow is the arrow to her next flag.
7. **The basement.** The camera sinks below the edge of the slab. The column carries straight on through the glass and down into the dark room, where it splashes a red disc on the distant floor, and the camera rises back home. *Twist:* The board has an underside nobody thought about, and the claim gets recorded on the floor of the room as well as on the board.
8. **The reflection celebrates.** At the claim Maya stays perfectly composed, but her reflection in the glass beneath her wiggles, bobs and twirls with joy. It freezes and lines back up the instant she tips her nose down to look. *Twist:* Robots don't celebrate, but apparently their reflections do, and from then on the room starts watching the reflections.
9. **Through a drinking glass.** For a beat the column condenses from light into a clear glass rod around her. Like a goldfish seen through a tumbler, Maya bulges wide and wobbly inside it and blinks her stretched eye once, then the rod melts back into light. *Twist:* Real refraction played for comedy: we are looking at her through the flag.
10. **The raft.** The claim gives the floating slab a weight it wasn't expecting: the corner under Maya dips, the whole board rocks gently like a raft taking on a passenger, Theo riding the swell, and it bobs back level. *Twist:* The slab remembers that it floats, and the flag makes her heavier.
11. **Diamond ring.** The camera drops almost to the glass so that Maya's dark silhouette blocks the bright foot of the column. A corona of light flares round her outline, and a single bright bead slides round it, an eclipse's "diamond ring". The bead drops onto the glass as her taken ring, and the camera drifts home. *Twist:* An astronomy pun the board can actually perform: the diamond ring becomes the ring.
12. **Euler's disk.** A ring of red light falls from the top of the column, hits the glass on its edge and spins down like a coin settling, wobbling faster and faster with a rising whirr until it lies flat with a click as the taken ring. *Twist:* It borrows the most satisfying desk-toy motion there is. The speeding wobble is a built-in drumroll with an exact ending.
13. **The boop.** The column bends over like a reed in the wind and taps Maya on the top. She squashes under the tap, and the red its tip picked up from her runs down the column like a drop down a straw into the ring. *Twist:* Maya doesn't reach the flag; the flag reaches her, with a friendly pat from the board.
14. **Rubber stamp.** The column shortens into a thick hollow tube of light and thumps down round her. Maya jumps off the glass with the impact, and when the tube lifts, a crisp red ring is stamped into the glass like a passport stamp. *Twist:* Bureaucratic comedy: her progress is officially stamped, with a soft glass thunk.
15. **Elevator.** Maya floats straight up inside the column and out of the top of the frame. The column hums, empty, for a beat, a soft lift chime sounds, and she comes down wearing the red ring like a lifebuoy. It slips off her onto the glass. *Twist:* The flag is a lift to somewhere we never see, and what happened up there is the room's to guess.
16. **Twin column.** Maya stretches up out of herself into a red column of her own inside the white one, as tall as the flag. The two sway together, then she snaps back down into a Pebble with a springy wobble, leaving the ring. *Twist:* Squash and stretch pushed until the robot becomes the landmark; for a beat she is the flag.
17. **Magnet.** On her last half-space Maya is yanked forward as if the flag were a magnet: a snap, a tiny overshoot past the centre, a springy settle back onto (4,1), and a clack. *Twist:* A one-frame accent small enough never to get old. It happens only when the space is your next flag (a flag out of order doesn't pull), which quietly teaches the order rule.
18. **The warm spot.** Maya settles into the column's warm light like a cat on a sunny windowsill. She flattens a little, her eye dims to half-closed, and a faint purr ripples through her ring before she perks back up to her normal shape. *Twist:* The column is warm white light and a Pebble is the shape of a curled-up cat. Nobody expects a robot to look content.
19. **The secret celebration.** At the claim the whole board freezes: Theo, the column's shimmer, the drifting motes. Only Maya moves, doing a tiny wiggling happy dance, then she stops, straightens up, and the world carries on as if nothing happened. *Twist:* The toys-come-alive trick turned inside out: she celebrates only when she thinks nobody is watching, and the whole room is.
20. **Slow clap.** Maya points herself at Theo for one deliberate beat (look what I've got). Theo's ring pulses three slow, flat times with three dry glass tinks, a sarcastic slow clap, and she turns back east. *Twist:* A rivalry told by two lights blinking. It reads from across the room and needs nothing new.
21. **Heads turn.** At the claim every other robot swings its pointer round to stare at her at the same instant, holds, then all snap back to their own facings a little too fast, like people caught staring. *Twist:* With six robots on a real board it's a whole library turning round, and the guilty snap back is the joke.
22. **The lamp across the board.** Flag 2's column bends at the middle like an anglepoise lamp and leans across the board towards Maya, its tip looking at her. It gives one small nod and springs back upright. *Twist:* The next flag has noticed her. The moment is told from where she's heading, and the room's eyes follow it there.
23. **Spin the wheel.** The column flickers through every player's colour like a slowing roulette wheel and lingers temptingly on blue: Theo's eye brightens and his pointer perks up. Then it clicks over to red, and Theo's light sags. *Twist:* Fake suspense about something everyone already knows, with the loser's hope as the punchline. It settles within 0.8 s, so nobody is confused.
24. **The sleepy flag.** Maya arrives and nothing happens: the column is dim and swaying slowly. She leans forward once, then again, and the column jolts awake with a startled flare and hurries to hand over her ring. *Twist:* The board element has a personality, a sleepy night-shift attendant, and the pause is comic timing.
25. **Six ways to say yes.** Each form claims a flag in its own way. The Pebble rocks like a stone settling in a riverbed. The Orb rolls a tiny loop round the column's foot. The Prism splits the column into a spectrum that fans across the glass. The Capsule stands on end and lies back down. The Cube tips over onto each side and back. The Spire stretches into a needle that threads the column. *Twist:* The forms get personalities, so after a few flags the room knows what Ava's claim will look like and calls it before it happens.
26. **Ticket punch.** The Move 2 card glyph that popped up above her drifts down into the column, which punches a round hole through it with a click. The punched-out disc of red light falls and lands as her ring, and the holed card flutters off and fades. *Twist:* The card that got her there takes the credit, and the ring is literally the chad from her ticket, a nod to the programming half of the game.
27. **Score track.** A small red bead zips out of her ring, drops over the edge of the slab and runs along its lit rim like a marker on a board game's score track. It stops at a notch halfway round, which glows for a moment and fades. *Twist:* The board's frame becomes a classic score track, so the race reads as one lap of the board, and the last flag would complete it.
28. **Token flip.** The glass tile under the flag flips over like a cardboard game token, with Maya stuck to it. For half a second she is upside down beneath the slab, seen through the glass as if she were her own reflection. Then the tile finishes its turn and lands her upright, her ring now showing on top. *Twist:* The board-game gesture of flipping a token to its claimed side, done in glass, and for a moment she swaps places with her reflection.
29. **The rulebook rewrites itself.** Thin type writes itself on the glass beside her: "Ending a register on your next flag claims it." The words "your next flag" glow red, get struck through and are rewritten as "flag 2", then the line fades. *Twist:* The rules are part of the scenery and edit themselves for her, which teaches new players the rule without a tutorial.
30. **The fraction.** A thin "1/2" in light hangs in the column above her. The 1 drops into her ring with a clink, and the "/2" floats off across the board into flag 2's column, which brightens a notch. *Twist:* The type does the bookkeeping: her score splits into what's done and what's left, and what's left goes where she needs to go next.
31. **Not yet... now.** When she arrives, the column only half notices: a faint red thread runs up it and holds there, trembling, for the rest of the register. On the checkpoint beat it commits, and the red climbs to the top and runs down into the ring. *Twist:* It teaches the real rule: you claim the flag by ending the register on it, after belts, pushers and lasers have had their turn. On a busy board that held breath becomes real suspense, and if she's carried off the flag the thread snaps back to white.
32. **Three-note approach.** Each space she crosses lights the seam under her and plays a rising note, and arriving on the flag plays the third note of the chord, which the column rings out. *Twist:* The move and the claim become one musical phrase, and the tune depends on the card: a Move 3 onto a flag plays four notes.
33. **The question and the answer.** Flag 1 sings a clear note as she claims it. A moment later flag 2, across the board, flickers and answers with a higher note that doesn't resolve, like a question. *Twist:* A cadence that lasts the whole game: it resolves only when someone claims the final flag and wins, so the room hears the race as a run of two-note phrases.
34. **Finger on the rim.** Maya's eye light runs once round her ring of light on the glass, like a wet finger round the rim of a wine glass. The column hums, rising in pitch and brightening, as the circle closes into her red ring. *Twist:* Lumen's glass sound made visible. The gesture is tiny, but the room hears the glass sing.
35. **Struck glass rod.** When she arrives, the column rings like a struck glass rod. It visibly shivers in a standing wave, each swing leaving a thin band of red on it, until it goes still with the red pooled at the bottom as her ring. *Twist:* The column is a physical object with a pitch, and the vibration that makes the sound also leaves the mark.
36. **Stadium wave.** The glass seams around the board light up one after another, and a Mexican wave of light rolls round the rim of the slab and back to her. *Twist:* The board itself is the crowd, and on a 12-space board the wave lasts long enough for the room to join in.
37. **Dolly zoom.** For half a second the camera does a Vertigo dolly zoom on Maya: she and the column stay the same size while the rest of the board stretches away into the dark, then everything eases back. *Twist:* Wildly overdramatic film language for a modest progress beat, short enough to be a joke and not a dizzy spell.
38. **The flag's point of view.** The camera cuts to the top of the column, looking straight down at Maya on the glass. She tips back and looks up into the lens, her eye pointing straight at the room, blinks once, and the camera cuts home. *Twist:* For one beat the TV is the flag and Maya is looking at the people on the sofa.
39. **Celebration in reverse.** Today's rings and motes play backwards: big red rings slide in from beyond the edges of the screen and close in on the flag, and motes fall out of the dark into the column. On the last frame it all gathers into her single ring. *Twist:* The usual burst becomes a gathering in, which is calmer and more Lumen and ends exactly on the thing that stays.
40. **Constellation.** The camera tilts up the column into the dark above the board, where its top is a single star. A thin line of light draws from it to a second star, the top of flag 2's column, joining the dots, and the camera drops back down. *Twist:* The dark room becomes a night sky, and her route through the course becomes a constellation that grows with each flag.
41. **Selfie.** Maya turns to face the room, the column behind her. Her eye flashes like a phone camera while Theo leans into the shot behind her, and the frozen frame shrinks to a thumbnail that tucks under her name tag as she turns back east. *Twist:* The robot photographs the people watching, and the photobomb is a second joke.
42. **Buffering.** At the claim a thin ring of light appears over the column and spins like a loading spinner, as if the TV had stalled waiting for a phone. After a few turns the claim arrives all at once with a chime. *Twist:* A joke at the expense of the game's own phone link that works because the room has seen real lag; best used once a game at most.
43. **The light leaves the TV.** The top of the column tips towards the camera and its light comes straight at the lens, washing the screen warm white from one side for a beat, as if the TV were shining into the living room at Maya's player. Then it swings back upright. *Twist:* The flag congratulates the human, not the robot, and in a dark living room the real room lights up for a second.
44. **The audience in the dark.** For a moment the dark room round the slab fills with dozens of small soft lights at different distances, in all the players' colours, swaying like phone screens held up at a concert, then they fade. *Twist:* The empty dark room was a stadium all along, and the lights are the colours of the actual people in the room.
45. **The detour.** Too eager, Maya shoots off diagonally towards flag 2, which is no legal move. Halfway there the glass plays a soft "nope" chime, and her trail reels her back like an elastic band onto flag 1, facing east. *Twist:* She breaks the rules in plain view and the rules undo it, which says "next: flag 2" better than any text.
46. **The eager flag.** Halfway through her move the column slides over to meet her on (3,1). She startles, and the two ride the last space together to (4,1), arriving as the column settles back on its own spot. *Twist:* The flag can't wait. Flags never move in this game, so the break is obvious, and it's put right before the claim counts.
47. **Victory lap, without her.** The trail she left lifts off the glass behind her and runs a whole lap round the edge of the slab on its own, a red streak racing the rim. It comes back and coils into the ring at her feet while Maya stays exactly where she is. *Twist:* Her trail is more excited than she is: the robot keeps her dignity while her light runs the lap for her.
48. **The standings know.** The column's reaction depends on the race. The first robot to reach a flag gets a full ring-down. A robot claiming it last, after everyone else's rings are stacked, gets a small sympathetic pat: the column's light dips gently towards her with a soft low note. *Twist:* The animation reads the standings, so no two claims look alike and the room learns to read pride and pity in the light.
49. **The tick.** For a few frames Maya's pointer, the little triangle that shows her facing, bends into a tick mark, then springs back into a triangle. *Twist:* A detail for the people watching closely. Once someone spots it, the whole room watches for it at every flag.
50. **Make a wish.** As she arrives, the column flickers like a candle flame. Her eye gives a little puff, the column goes out, and a thin curl of smoke made of light rises. After a beat of darkness the column relights warm white with her ring at its foot. *Twist:* Birthday-candle logic, plus a split second where the flag seems gone for everyone, before it comes back because the others still need it.
