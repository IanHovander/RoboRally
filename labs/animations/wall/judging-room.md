# Wall: the room's verdict

Judged as six friends on a couch watching the TV between turns. Bumping into a wall is common (it happens whenever
someone miscounts), so it may play 10 to 20 times in a night. The budget is 2.5 s, and every entry's moment fits it
(2.2 to 2.47 s). Today the game simply stops her at the fin in 0.87 s.

## Winner: 1b-revolving-door (the hopeful one)

**Why.** This is the one the couch shouts "again!" at. One big, simple swing round the fin reads from anywhere in the
room, and the held "I made it!" pop on the far side lands the joke before the door whisks her back. Geometry keeps
the rule (a full turn is no move), she ends nose to the fin facing east, and the ring of light left round the fin
tells anyone who looked away what happened. Spinning once per lost step keeps it varied and quietly teaches the rule.

**Port: fix or watch.**
- **Swing arc.** Check it before playing. She sweeps through the edges of the squares beside the fin, so another
  robot or wall there, or a fin on the board edge, needs a fallback (a tighter swing or a plain bump).
- **Time budget.** Cap the time per extra turn so a Move 3 that loses every step still fits 2.5 s.
- **Ring.** Fade the ring on the normal trail clock.
- **Ava.** Make her reaction rare or drop it. A bystander that turns and rocks on every miscount gets old and
  briefly suggests another robot moved.
- **Ending.** End on 1a's heavier lock clunk and spring-back.

## Runner-up: 1a-revolving-door (heavy glass)

**Why.** The same gag played as pure weight: she hits the fin at speed, it creaks, swings her round and slams into
its lock while she skids and springs back. Only Maya and the door move, so it is the clearest and most repeatable
version. It just rushes the far-side beat that makes 1b funnier.

**Port: fix or watch.**
- **Same checks as the winner.** Robots or walls beside the fin, fins on the board edge, and the time budget for
  several turns.
- **Far-side hold.** Hold the far side for a few more frames so the room registers she got through.
- **Sound.** Keep the creak, whoosh and clunk, which carry the joke when the robot is small on a busy board.

Best of the rest: 2c-to-camera gets the loudest single laugh, and 5c-lost-and-found is the biggest surprise. Both
lose points on repeat because the camera leaves its home on every miscount.
