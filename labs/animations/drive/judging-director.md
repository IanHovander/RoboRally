## Winner: 4c-light-painted-ribbon

It is the only entry that makes every drive better at no cost in time: it plays in 2.97 s against today's 3.03 s, and it is built only from Lumen's own trail. Instead of adding noise it adds information: a curl or a bow left on the glass tells anyone glancing up late where a robot turned or U-turned. The bow is a small smile that still works the hundredth time.

**Porting:** Replace updateTrail with a ribbon the scene owns: the trail's points, plus a curl per quarter turn and a loop and bow for a U-Turn, straightened by the next move. Fix the Move Back tug so the ribbon keeps the true corner at (3, 2) instead of cutting a diagonal from just left of it. Drop the star glint on the bow and the white bloom on her body at the start of the whip. Fade the bow and curls on the trail's 3.2 s schedule, and clear them on a fall or reboot. Only her own turn cards should curl the ribbon, never gear, belt or push rotations. Check that Rotate Left mirrors the curl, that back-to-back turns don't pile into a knot, and that the ribbon is wide enough to read on a 13-space board in every player colour.

## Runner-up: 2a-claw-machine

This is the loudest moment in the set, the one most likely to get an 'again!', and of the three claws it has the best craft. It has a clear claw silhouette in warm light, weight in the cable bounce, strain and pendulum lag, a slip that earns a gasp, and a drop that lands exactly on (3, 1). It breaks the look on purpose but is made of light and gone before the U-Turn ends. As a once-or-twice-a-game treat it pairs well with the everyday ribbon.

**Porting:** Trigger it rarely, seeded by the host so every screen agrees, and only on a Move Back with open air above. It uses nearly the whole budget (a 4.40 s moment against 4.5 s), so never stack it with other slow beats in the same register. Fix the one-frame ring blink at about 4.40 s. Ease the claw's exit over about 0.3 s instead of 0.1 s, and shrink the white impact ring to the game's normal ring size so it isn't read as a status effect. Hold the played card at its usual screen height: here it rides up to the top edge of the frame during the carry. Drive the swing from the Move Back direction (opposite her facing), not a fixed north, and keep drawing no trail for the carried space.
