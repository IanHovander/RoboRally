# Reboot: 50 animation ideas (Lumen)

Maya (red Pebble) comes back on the Reboot token at (3,1), facing east along its arrow, and takes 2 SPAM. Budget 3 s.
Every idea ends with her resting there, Theo untouched at (5,2) facing west, the camera home and nothing left over.

## Banned (too obvious)

- A teleporter beam: a column of light drops from above and she materialises inside it (today's pillar).
- Scaling up from a point with an elastic overshoot (today's pop).
- Boot-up clichés: a loading bar or spinner, "REBOOTING..." / "SYSTEM ONLINE" type, a startup chime.
- A digital glitch-in: pixels, static, scanlines, a hologram flicker.
- Sparks or shards flying off her for the 2 damage (today's shards).
- Camera shake on landing.
- A glow burst or shockwave ring spreading out from the token.
- Dropping from the sky and landing with a thud and a puff of dust.
- Rising out of the floor like an elevator or through a trapdoor.
- A generic spin into place before she settles facing east.
- A floating "+2 SPAM", skull or warning triangle over her head.
- Blue screen and "have you tried turning it off and on again" jokes.

## Ideas

1. **Her reflection gets there first.** Under the glass at the token, a red upside-down Maya fades up as if reflected in the smoked glass, though nothing stands above it yet. The reflection then flips up through the surface like a card turned face-up and the real Maya takes its place with one chime, while two ripples (the 2 SPAM) wobble her reflection before it settles. *Twist:* the glass knew she was back before she was, and the reflection, usually a background detail, gets to be the star for a beat.
2. **Theo the prism, by accident.** A thread of warm white light slides in out of the dark east of the board, strikes Theo's faceted body and fans into a spectrum across the glass. Every band fades except the red one, which lands on the token and pools upward into Maya, and two grey bands slip in after it (the damage). *Twist:* the Prism form finally does what prisms do, so her rival builds her without meaning to, and his eye does a small "wait, what?" glance afterwards.
3. **The light drains to her.** The glass dims from its edges inward as the board's light flows like water toward a drain and gathers in one bright red pool on the token. The pool stands up as Maya, the board's light flows back out to the edges, and two drops come back grey and soak into her (the damage). *Twist:* a pit in Lumen is where the light is missing, so a reboot is the opposite, the one spot where all the light collects, and you only notice it because everywhere else goes dark for a second.
4. **A hole that fills.** First a perfect Pebble-shaped hole of darkness opens in the token's light, her exact silhouette with eye, ring and pointer all cut out. Red seeps into the hole from the bottom like metal filling a mould and rises into her solid form, and two grey blots blink out as it sets (the damage). *Twist:* she comes back as an absence first, which is how Lumen draws a pit, so one image says both "she fell" and "she's back".
5. **Signed back in.** A point of red light skates over the glass and draws her outline in one continuous calligraphic stroke, finishing with a flick that becomes the east arrow. The drawing inflates into the polished robot and the leftover ink fades as an ordinary trail. *Twist:* she's drawn back with the same light her trails are made of, like a long-exposure light painting, and the stroke wobbles twice where the 2 SPAM land, a shaky signature.
6. **Clear glass first, colour last.** Maya arrives as perfectly clear glass, visible only because the board's seams bend through her. Then her red drips in from the top like dye in water, curling down until she's fully coloured, except for two grey threads that won't dissolve and sink into her base (the damage). *Twist:* for half a second the room sees a ghost made out of the board itself, and her colour coming in reads as her coming to.
7. **Fresh from the glassblower.** A molten gather of white-hot glass sits on the token, turning as if on an invisible pipe. It swells with two breaths, gets shaped, cools from white through amber to her red, and finally its nose is pinched to point east, with two tiny bubbles trapped inside that glint and fade (the 2 SPAM: a glassblower's flaws). *Twist:* it answers "where do Lumen's robots come from?": they're made of the game's one material, and the damage is a flaw in the glass, not an explosion.
8. **Constellation.** Faint star-points pick themselves out in the dark above the board and join with thin lines into a little constellation in her shape. The constellation drops as a single streak and lands on the token as Maya, and two stars fall in late and dim behind her (the damage). *Twist:* she wasn't gone, she was up in the dark being remembered, and it's the one falling star that ends in a soft landing instead of burning up.
9. **Lighthouse.** Only her eye comes back at first, high above the token, sweeping a slow warm beam across the dark room. The beam catches haze, the slab's edges and Theo, who dims his ring as if squinting, then it sputters twice (the damage), stops dead on east, and her body condenses behind it and lowers onto the token. *Twist:* her facing isn't snapped into place, it's searched for, and the room watches the beam choose the arrow.
10. **Splash crown.** A bead of red light falls out of the dark onto the token, and for a heartbeat the glass behaves like water: rings spread, the grid lines bend, Theo bobs. The jet that leaps up out of the splash is Maya, who drops back onto glass that's still again, and two last droplets plink beside her and soak in (the damage). *Twist:* it's the slow-motion water-drop shot from every nature film, with the robot as the jet.
11. **A belt just for her.** For two seconds a private stream of light switches on under the glass, running from the board's east edge to the token, and carries a small parcel of red light along it like luggage on an airport carousel. The parcel stops on the token and unfolds into Maya, two grey parcels follow and bump into her back (the damage), and the stream switches off. *Twist:* the board builds and takes down a belt for one passenger, and repeat viewers notice it always runs in from the edge she fell off.
12. **Reeled in.** The faded trail she left on the way to her fall flares back up and retracts like a tape measure, a ribbon of red light zipping in across the glass from beyond the board's edge. Maya arrives on the end of it, snaps onto the token like a tape measure's tab, and two kinks in the ribbon jolt her (the damage). *Twist:* the trail, Lumen's most decorative touch, turns out to have been a lifeline she was tied to the whole time.
13. **Focus pull.** A big soft red disc of out-of-focus light hangs over the token as if the camera can't find her. The camera racks focus, the board softens then sharpens, and the disc tightens into a crisp Maya, hunting twice (each a brief blur: the 2 SPAM) before it locks. *Twist:* the reboot is a cinematographer's focus pull, and her glow pool is all that's left of the blur.
14. **Euler's disk.** Maya arrives tilted onto her rim, rolling round and round on the token like a coin settling on a table. The wobble gets smaller and faster as a glass whirr rises in pitch, stutters twice (the damage), and she claps flat with her nose exactly east. *Twist:* it's real physics with a payoff the room learns to anticipate: the faster it goes, the closer the end, and the end always lands on the arrow.
15. **Curling stone.** Maya glides in from the board's edge like a curling stone, turning slowly with a long hiss and a fading trail, and drifts to a stop dead centre in the token's ring as if it were the house. Theo's eye tracks her the whole way, and she rocks on two seams as she crosses them (the damage). *Twist:* the Pebble is shaped like a curling stone and the reboot ring is drawn like the house, which nobody planned and everyone notices.
16. **Skipping stone.** She skims in low and fast from the east edge and skips across the glass three times, each touch leaving a ring that bends the grid lines, each skip shorter than the last. The last two splashes come up grey (the damage), and the final skip drops her onto the token, where she slides the last inch to rest facing east. *Twist:* the Pebble is a stone and the glass is a lake for a second, and the room starts counting the skips.
17. **Shakes it off.** Maya appears coated in darkness, dull and unlit as if just pulled out of the pit, and shakes from nose to tail like a wet dog. The darkness flies off in black droplets that spatter the glass and evaporate to reveal her red, and two of them stick and blink grey before fading (the damage). *Twist:* Lumen's darkness becomes something you can be soaked in, and the shake is pure dog.
18. **Junk food.** Maya blinks back quietly, then two small dull-grey glass tiles (her SPAM cards) drift down out of the dark toward her. She swallows them one at a time, gulp and gulp, each one bulging down through her body and turning her paint queasy for a moment. *Twist:* damage becomes something she's made to eat, which is exactly what SPAM does to her hand, and the second gulp is visibly more reluctant.
19. **Overshoot.** She launches off the token with far too much energy and vanishes upward out of frame. The board sits empty for a full beat while Theo's eye glances up, then she drifts down slowly, rocking side to side like a falling leaf, and bumps twice on the token (the damage) before settling facing east. *Twist:* the joke is the pause, and the leaf-fall is the gentlest thing Lumen ever does to her.
20. **Inflatable.** She arrives flat, a deflated red sheet of light lying on the token, and inflates in three pumps, each with a small squeak, until she overshoots into a too-round balloon. Two hisses of air (the damage) let her down to her true shape. *Twist:* it's squash and stretch as body comedy, and the damage literally lets the air out of her.
21. **Dandelion seed.** She drifts down under a small dome of warm-white filaments like a dandelion seed, swaying, with two filaments snapping and fading grey on the way (the damage). As she touches down, the dome collapses flat onto the glass and becomes her ring of light. *Twist:* the parachute was her ring all along, so nothing is left over and nothing new was added.
22. **Everyone's late.** Her parts turn up separately like latecomers: the ring of light skates in from one side, the eye blinks on in the air above it, and the pointer arrives facing the wrong way and has to swing round to east. Last comes her body, hurrying, which bumps into its own pointer twice (the damage) and drops into place. *Twist:* like Peter Pan's shadow, the bits of her that are normally stuck to her get a life of their own for a moment.
23. **Where am I?** A single warm-white point of light, her eye, blinks on in the empty air above the token. It looks left, then right, then at Theo, blinks twice groggily (the damage), and only then does her body condense behind it and settle onto the token facing east. *Twist:* one dot of light and two glances give her a whole personality, and the room reads "dazed" from across the room.
24. **Jump-start.** Theo turns his head slightly and throws two thin lines of light like jumper cables onto the token. One pulse runs along them and nothing happens, a second pulse and she coughs into being, then coughs twice more in small grey puffs (the damage), the cables retract and Theo turns back to face west. *Twist:* her rival helps her out, and the damage is just her engine catching.
25. **Saved you a seat.** While the token is still empty, Theo's glow pool stretches across the glass and drapes over it, like a towel thrown over a sun lounger. When Maya appears, it snaps back to Theo with an elastic twang, she flinches twice at the snap (the damage), and Theo's eye looks pointedly away. *Twist:* it's bystander comedy that bends nothing, since only Theo's light moved, never Theo.
26. **Theo goes fishing.** Theo turns and casts his laser off the board's edge like a fishing line, a slow curving arc of light with a bobber at the end. There's a tug, he reels Maya in out of the dark, she lands on the token and flops twice like a landed fish (the damage), and Theo turns back west as if nothing happened. *Twist:* the beam that normally hurts people does a rescue, and it's the only time a laser ever curves in Lumen.
27. **Six forms, six reboots.** Every form has its own signature return: the Pebble skips in like a stone, the Orb bounces in and settles into its ring, the Prism is refracted together, the Capsule splits open like a pill, the Cube unfolds from a flat net on the glass, and the Spire grows up from a point like a crystal. *Twist:* over a game night the table learns everyone's reboot, so a player's form becomes a promise of how their disaster will end.
28. **Shell game.** Three upturned glass domes slide out of the dark, one over the token, and shuffle around each other in quick swaps. They lift and Maya is under the one that stopped on the token, while the other two hold dull grey tiles that slide over and into her (the damage). *Twist:* the room shouts guesses, and it's always the token, because that's the rule, but the shuffle is different every time.
29. **Wound up.** The circular-arrow glyph on the token turns out to be a wind-up key: it lifts out of the glass and turns with three ratchet clicks, the last two sour (the damage). Maya pops up on it like a wind-up toy and waddles a small circle on the spot until her pointer lines up with the arrow and the key winds down into the glass. *Twist:* the symbol the board has shown all game was a key the whole time.
30. **The rule writes her.** A line of thin type writes itself across the glass beside the token: "Reboot: back to the token, take 2 damage". Then the letters slide together onto the token and fold up into Maya, with "2 damage" going in last as two dull glyphs that sink into her. *Twist:* the rulebook literally builds her, so new players learn the rule from the animation and nobody has to explain it.
31. **Sliding puzzle.** The board's light seams brighten so the spaces read as tiles, and the token's tile drops a hair and slides away under its neighbours like a 15-puzzle. The empty tiles shuffle in a quick clacking cascade (Theo's stays put), and the token tile slides back in with Maya riding on top, jolted twice as the last tiles clack home (the damage). *Twist:* the board rearranges itself to deliver her, and every tile lands back exactly where it started.
32. **Labyrinth.** The view tips like a wooden marble labyrinth tilted by an unseen player. Maya rolls in off the board's edge, steered left and right as the tilt corrects, and drops into the token like a marble into the goal, rattling twice (the damage) as the table levels out. *Twist:* someone in the room is "playing" the board; the camera's tilt does all the work, so only Maya moves, while Theo sits tight with a nervous eye.
33. **Parking.** She arrives with too much speed, and the token's nudge rule fires on her: the arrow shoves her one space east, off the token. She pauses, then reverses slowly back onto it with a soft beeping chime, still facing east, and bumps the kerb twice as she parks (the damage). *Twist:* the nudge, meant for a robot already sitting on the token, misfires on the one robot it's for, and she fixes it like a parallel park.
34. **Claw machine.** A claw drawn in thin lines of light comes down from the top of the frame, past the board's edge, into the dark below, and comes back up with Maya dangling and wobbling dangerously. It swings over the token and drops her, and she clunks down twice (the damage) and sits. *Twist:* it's the arcade claw that always drops the prize, except this one drops her exactly on the token, so the room ends up cheering for a claw machine.
35. **Glass harp.** A point of light runs around the rim of the slab like a wet finger round a wine glass, faster and faster, while the hum climbs and the whole glass faintly shimmers. At the peak the token resonates, Maya rings into being on one clear note, and two sour overtones shiver through her (the damage). *Twist:* the board is a wine glass, and you hear her come back before you see her.
36. **Count-in.** The token's ring becomes a clock face: three soft ticks each light a third of it, like a conductor counting in. On the downbeat she's there, then two off-beat notes come in flat and she flinches on each (the damage). *Twist:* the damage is literally out of tune, so the room hears what SPAM means without any glyph.
37. **Her fall, played backwards.** The falling glass notes from her fall replay in reverse, rising, and each note lays a thin red ring of light on top of the last, stacking up into her silhouette like a xylophone made of light. The last two notes are sour and their rings land crooked before snapping straight (the damage), and the stack fuses into her. *Twist:* falling and returning are the same tune, and anyone who heard the fall recognises it.
38. **Double take.** The camera glances at the empty token, then drifts toward Theo as if bored. Then it whips back to find Maya sitting there as if she always had been, giving the camera two innocent blinks (the damage). *Twist:* the camera missed it; she slipped back while nobody was looking, which is funnier than any effect.
39. **The board reboots instead.** The whole room goes dark, glass, seams, Theo's glow, everything, leaving only Theo's eye in the black for a beat. Then the board's light comes back in a sweep from one edge, space by space like a city's power returning, stuttering twice as it passes the token (the damage), and Maya is there. *Twist:* it isn't Maya that got turned off and on again, it's the whole game.
40. **Late for her cue.** The room dims and a warm follow-spot snaps on over the empty token; a beat passes and the spot fidgets. Maya hurries in from the board's edge, overshoots, stumbles twice shuffling back (the damage), and the spot shrinks down to become her ring of light. *Twist:* the theatre is ready and the star is late, and the spotlight's impatience is the gag.
41. **Thrown back in from the sofa.** She arcs up from the bottom of the screen, from the room's side, over the HUD, in a high lob as if someone on the sofa had tossed her back onto the table. She lands on the token with a bounce and two rattles (the damage). *Twist:* it suggests she fell out of the TV into the living room and the players threw her back.
42. **Out of the HUD.** Her little chip in the HUD at the bottom of the screen, where she waited while she was out, lights up and jumps out of the flat interface into the 3D world, flying up to the token and landing as the robot. Two grey SPAM cards then drop from her back down into the chip. *Twist:* the flat HUD and the 3D world are one place for a moment, and it shows the player exactly where to look for their damage.
43. **Maya has rejoined.** A flat grey tile with a big "M" stands on the token like a video-call participant with their camera off. It turns "camera on" with a two-tone chime and pops into 3D as Maya, who freezes twice with an "unstable connection" stutter (the damage) before she's steady. *Twist:* everyone in the room has lived through this exact moment in a meeting.
44. **Fogged screen.** A soft patch of the TV picture over the token fogs milky, as if someone in the room had breathed on the screen. A fingertip line doodles her shape and an arrow in the mist, with two smudges (the damage), and as the fog clears the doodle becomes her. *Twist:* the TV is glass too, and it's doodled the way friends at a table draw on things.
45. **The damage comes to you.** Maya reboots quietly, then two grey SPAM cards fly out of her straight at the camera, growing as if they're about to come out of the TV into the room. They peel off toward the bottom of the screen, where her player's phone would be. *Twist:* the damage visibly goes where it really goes, into the player's hand on the phone in the room.
46. **Slow clap.** As she appears, faint glows in every other player's colour come up around the edges of the dark room and pulse slowly together: clap... clap... clap. The last two claps land on her as grey flashes (the damage). *Twist:* the darkness around the board, usually empty, turns out to be an audience, and it's sarcastic.
47. **Junk copies.** Maya arrives with two pale, wrong-coloured copies of herself standing slightly offset, like a double exposure. All three look at each other, then the copies slide into her with one shudder each (the damage). *Twist:* the 2 SPAM are literally junk copies of her shoved into her deck, and repeat viewers spot what's wrong with them (an eye in the wrong place, a dull ring).
48. **The token keeps count.** Every reboot this game has scratched a tiny notch of light into the token's ring, like tally marks on a cell wall. As Maya arrives the old notches light up one by one and a new one is scratched in with a squeak of glass, and she flinches at the scratch twice (the damage). *Twist:* the board remembers, and by the end of the night the most-used token is visibly scratched up and players point at it.
49. **Worse every time.** The reboot gets worse each time the same player falls: the first is graceful, the second ends in a sagging sigh, the third has her lowered on a string of light, the fourth fetched by a claw with a "here we go again" wobble, and the 2-damage beat gets more theatrical each time. *Twist:* it's a running gag for the whole night, and the room laughs before it lands because they know what's coming.
50. **Sewn back on.** A needle of red light pokes up through the glass near the board's edge and stitches toward the token, dipping in and out of the surface and leaving a dashed thread. At the token the thread pulls tight and gathers into Maya, with two stitch holes glowing on her ring for a moment (the damage). *Twist:* she's sewn back onto the board, and Lumen's trail of light turns out to be thread.

## The five to animate

1. **3. The light drains to her** (the gasp): the whole board goes dim and pours its light into the token. It is the most readable picture from the back of the room, and calm enough to see a few times a game.
2. **16. Skipping stone** (the shout): the room counts the skips out loud, a ritual that improves with repetition, and the last two grey ripples are the damage.
3. **17. Shakes it off** (the laugh): a black silhouette shaking like a wet dog reads in pure silhouette and motion, and says "she fell" and "she's fine" in one gesture.
4. **26. Theo goes fishing** (the rivalry): the nearest robot reels her in on a slack, curving laser, so two players react, and the rescuer is different every time.
5. **44. Fogged screen** (the surprise): the TV's own glass fogs over and a fingertip doodles her. The fourth wall breaks for two seconds, and the picture comes home to Lumen.
