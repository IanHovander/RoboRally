# Reboot: the room's verdict

Judged as six friends on a couch watching the TV between turns. Reboot happens once for every fall, a few times a game, so a whole evening sees it many times. Scores are 1 to 10, and overall is a judgement, not an average. Each entry was judged from its contact sheet and from frames pulled from the video; the baseline (a ring pulse and fade-in) is the bar.

## Winner: 4c-night-fishing

It gets the best reaction from the room. Another player's robot does the rescue, so two people react at once: Theo's player takes a bow and Maya's player gets teased, and because the nearest robot does the fishing, the joke changes hands from reboot to reboot. Of the three fishing versions, this one stages the gag best for a TV: the cast is a big arc you can follow, the bite throws rings of light on the dark 'water', and Maya rises out of them, so the joke is built from Lumen's own light and ends with her clearly on the token facing east and Theo exactly where he was.

**Porting:** Fix Maya's name tag overlapping her body as she lands. Borrow 4b's beats (the bite double-take, her glare, Theo looking away) and stage them big enough to read from the couch. Keep the line clearly not a shot: no hit flash on contact, and grey her eye only on the flops after the line has retracted, so nobody thinks Theo dealt the 2 SPAM. Keep the camera ease gentle, skip it when that edge is already in frame, and make sure it is home before the round goes on. Drop the bead off the edge nearest where she fell. Decide what happens with no robot on the board, or when several robots reboot in the same round (common): stagger the casts or let one robot fish twice, and never pull the camera toward two edges at once. Watch the 2.8 s length when several reboots happen in a row.

## Runner-up: 1c-iris-drain

It gets the gasp: the board's own light drains toward the token and every eye follows it, so this is the clearest picture of where she comes back, and it is Lumen's idea that light is presence turned into motion. It is calm rather than a joke, so it suits a room that wants a breath of beauty after a cruel fall, and it scales to several reboots at once by splitting the streams between their tokens.

**Porting:** Make the streams look like light, not flat planks: soft additive ribbons with tapered ends. Don't black the board out completely; keep a faint outline so the board stays present. Make the lean optional or smaller, since a camera dive a few times a game, every game, adds up, and with several tokens at once the camera should stay home. Make the two grey threads and the eye dimming visible from the couch, or let the damage UI carry the 2 SPAM.

## Also worth keeping

3b-shakes-it-off holds up best of all over repeats: the camera stays home, it's quick, it's funny, and it reads clearly. It is the safe fallback for when no robot is near enough to fish. 4a's sagging rope physics and 4b's acting beats are worth folding into the fishing port.
