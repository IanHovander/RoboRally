# Push: the room's verdict

Judged as six friends on a couch watching the TV between turns. A push happens several times a game, so every entry has to hold up 10 to 20 times in a night. I looked at every contact sheet and pulled frames from every video. Today's game (baseline) is a plain 0.87 s slide. Every entry here runs 1.9 to 2.5 s, inside the 2.5 s budget.

Scores are 1 to 10. Overall is my judgement, not an average.

## Winner: 3c-femto-prism

It is the one that makes the couch go 'ooh' on the first push and still looks lovely on the tenth: Lumen's single warm light splitting into colour is the most striking image in the field, and it is made of nothing but light and glass. The freeze also does the game's job better than anything else here, because the rainbow ring is painted on the landing space before Theo moves, so nobody misses who shoved whom or where he ended up.

**Porting:** This moment happens several times a game, so cut the freeze from about 0.9 s to about 0.5 s on ordinary pushes, and ease the 0.2 s push-in (it feels like a jump). Keep the full lean-in for the first push of a game, chain pushes and shoves toward a pit or the edge. Keep the pusher's name tag on screen while the iris is closed. Swap the stock four-point star glint for a Lumen-native flare. The rainbow is only right for a Prism, so the Orb, Cube, Capsule, Spire and Pebble versions are separate pieces of work that need their own tests. Also handle chain pushes (one ring per robot) and a wall-blocked push, where the light should hit the wall fin and paint no ring.

## Runner-up: 3a-tape-measure

It is the same refraction idea at everyday weight: no camera move, a bright rainbow fan that reads at normal framing, and a slosh, reel-in and hiccup that add a small physical laugh. Of all the entries it is the safest to watch fifteen times a night, and it is the fallback if 3c's freeze proves too slow at the table.

**Porting:** Theo turns white glass at contact and loses his blue for about half a second, so keep a tint of the player colour in him. The fan is small at game framing, so borrow 3c's rainbow ring on the landing space to make the destination unmistakable. The hiccup hop is tiny, so either make it bigger or drop it. As with 3c, the other five forms each need their own way of bending the light.

## Also worth keeping

5b-theo-cam got the biggest laugh in the room, from Theo looking into the lens and Maya flashing her high beams. It cannot carry an everyday push, but as the rare one-in-ten variant it is the strongest of its kind. It needs the HUD hidden during the dive, which 5c already does.
