# Riding a conveyor belt: 50 animation ideas (Lumen)

Maya (red Pebble) starts on the express belt at (1,1) facing north. The express step carries her to (2,1), the all-belts step carries her to the corner at (3,1), and the corner turns her right. She ends at (3,1) facing east; the belt carries on south toward us, and Jun (yellow Cube) watches from (5,2), facing west. Budget: 2.5 s. It happens every register, so most of these are meant to vary or to stay small, and the big ones are marked as rare.

## Banned (too obvious)

- Speed lines or motion streaks behind her.
- The chevrons just flashing brighter or scrolling faster (today's pulse, turned up).
- A generic spin or pirouette at the corner.
- Sparks, dust or friction puffs under her base.
- A camera that follows her along the belt.
- Camera shake or a whoosh-zoom when she arrives.
- A glow burst or a ring pop when she reaches the corner.
- A "Whee!", an arrow glyph or a "+2" text pop.
- Roller-coaster hands up, or a surfer's lean.
- Treadmill jiggle: bouncing on rollers, clanking gears.
- The sushi-train or luggage-carousel gag played straight.

## Ideas

1. **Wrong-Way Rider.** Maya is still facing north when the stream takes her, so she's carried sideways, leaning stubbornly north while her ring smears east. At the corner two fins of light rise out of the belt's side rails, take her by the shoulders and turn her to face east like a parent steering a toddler, then sink back. *Twist:* the belt's own rails become hands, and the correction is gentle and slightly patronising: "you're going this way now."

2. **Wait, There's More.** On the express step she slides one space, stops and visibly relaxes (squashes down, eye dims to a content glow). The all-belts step yanks her again mid-sigh; she lurches back, is carried to the corner and turned, and stays a little stiff with her eye wide. *Twist:* the gag is the real rule (blue belts move once, then all belts move again), so the room learns "blue means two" by watching her get caught out.

3. **Forgot to Turn.** She arrives on the corner still facing north, and nothing happens for a long 0.4 s. Then the curved chevron under her brightens, like someone clearing their throat, and she gets a small, apologetic nudge round to east with a soft overshoot. *Twist:* timing alone makes the corner rule visible as its own separate step, and the pause gets the laugh.

4. **Lazy River.** Her ring of light lifts off the glass and inflates into an inner tube; she leans back in it and drifts along the stream, bobbing, bumps softly against the outside of the bend, and the bump swings her round to east as the tube shrinks back into a ring. *Twist:* the ring she always wears was secretly a pool float, and the turn comes from bumping the bend, as it would on a real river.

5. **Hamster Ball.** A sphere of smoked glass closes around her and rolls east, its highlights tumbling while she stays upright inside with her eye wobbling. At the corner the ball spins in place, then dissolves into glints and leaves her facing east. *Twist:* Lumen's one material becomes a toy, and the reflections roll while she doesn't.

6. **Zip Line.** A thread of warm-white light snaps taut from above her to above the corner; she's hoisted a little and zips along it, dangling. When the line stops she swings forward like a pendulum, the swing carries her round to east, and she drops onto the corner as the line frays into motes. *Twist:* the turn is the swing of a pendulum, so the rule looks like physics.

7. **Crowd Surf.** The chevrons under the glass rise through it as small raised fins of light, lift her and pass her along hand to hand for two spaces. At the corner the curved chevron twists her to east, sets her down, and everything sinks back into the stream. *Twist:* the belt's own arrows are the crowd at a gig, which suits a party game.

8. **Rally Drift.** Travelling east while facing north is already a drift, so she commits: she rolls into the slide, leaves two parallel tyre marks of red light instead of one trail, and at the corner counter-steers neatly into east as the marks curve with her. *Twist:* the start state is the joke (it is Robo *Rally*), and the tyre marks are just Lumen's trail split in two.

9. **Ring Runs After.** The belt takes her body but leaves her ring of light behind on the start square. It waits a beat, chases after her along the stream like a puppy, overshoots, scurries back and curls round her at the corner with its pointer arriving east. *Twist:* the most neutral part of a robot gets a personality, and since it carries the facing pointer, the turn reads through it.

10. **Skipping Stone.** She's a Pebble, so she skips: two low hops along the belt, one per beat, and each touchdown sends a ring rippling through the stream under the glass. The last skip curls round the bend and settles her facing east while the ripples fade in the corner. *Twist:* the form's name is the joke, and the two belt beats become two skips the room can count out loud.

11. **The Commute.** Robots get bored of belts over a game. On the first ride she's wide-eyed; by the fifth, Maya's eye dims to a slow breathing glow as she dozes in the flow, and the jolt of the corner wakes her with a start (eye snaps bright, quick squash), facing east. *Twist:* the delight is in the variation: the most frequent moment in the game gets sleepier as the night goes on, like a commute (the lab shows the dozing version).

12. **Phone Zombie.** While she's carried, a tiny lit rectangle hovers in front of her eye and the eye tips down to it, like someone scrolling on a moving walkway. The corner turns her without her looking up, and the rectangle blinks out. *Twist:* the robot is doing exactly what the players in the room are doing while the board plays.

13. **Indicator.** Half a space before the corner, a small warm light on her right flank blinks twice, tick, tock, and then she turns right. *Twist:* she politely signals a turn she has no control over; once someone spots it, the room watches for it on every ride (the light is on the left for left-hand corners).

14. **The Look.** At the corner she turns the long way round, left through west until she faces the camera, holds a deadpan stare at the room for a third of a second, then carries on round to east. *Twist:* a fourth-wall glance like the ones in The Office, and it still ends on the right heading; keep it rare so it stays funny.

15. **Eye Contact.** The turn leaves her facing east, across at Jun, who faces west. The moment her heading settles, both eyes flare and both pointers twitch, and a hairline of warm light flickers between them for a heartbeat. *Twist:* the belt didn't just turn her, it set up a staring contest; this works whenever a belt leaves one robot facing another.

16. **Marshal Jun.** Jun, the yellow Cube across the way, raises two little wands of light and marshals her in like airport ground crew: waving her on along the belt, sweeping one wand to "turn right" at the corner, then crossing them for "stop". *Twist:* a bystander takes a job, and the rule (go two, turn right, stop) is acted out by someone else's robot, which never leaves its own space.

17. **Curious Eye.** As she's carried east while facing north, her eye light slides round her body to peek in the direction she's going, like someone looking over their shoulder. The turn brings her front round to meet it, and the eye settles back into place. *Twist:* a tiny detail with a whole personality: she wants to see where she's being taken, and the turn reads as "finally facing where I was looking".

18. **Six Riders.** Each Lumen form rides belts its own way, so a busy register is a parade. The Orb rolls backwards against the flow like a ball on a treadmill, the Capsule rolls like a log, the Prism tilts its lit ridge like a sail, the Cube slides stiffly and squeaks to a stop, the Spire sways like a mast, and the Pebble skims (idea 10). *Twist:* the form each player picked shows up in a moment they don't control, and every form still lands square on its space, turned by the corner.

19. **Ink in the Stream.** Her colour bleeds into the belt under her like a drop of ink in a current. The red runs ahead of her through the corner and on down the south leg to the end of the belt, then fades as she settles on the corner facing east. *Twist:* it's a forecast as well as a pretty effect, showing where the belt will take her next register.

20. **Trail Goes With the Flow.** Her trail of light doesn't fade where she left it: the belt carries it too. The red streak drifts along after her, bends round the corner and flows away down the south leg, thinning to nothing. *Twist:* even the light obeys the belt; on a busy board the belts become rivers of everyone's trails, a detail that rewards anyone watching closely.

21. **Zen Garden.** The lines of light in the stream part around her base like raked gravel around a stone and close up behind her as she's carried. At the corner they settle in concentric arcs around her, then relax back into chevrons. *Twist:* a Pebble in a raked garden, the calmest and most Lumen way to say "the floor is moving, not her".

22. **The Koi.** A long, soft shape of light lives in the belts, seen through the smoked glass. When the belts run it glides under her and carries her on its back, then flicks its tail at the corner, which turns her east, and slips away down the south leg. *Twist:* a creature lives in the board's light; it appears only now and then, so the room starts watching the belts for it.

23. **Submerge.** She sinks into the glass to her middle, as if the stream were water, and rides along inside the slab, dimmed, tinted blue by the belt and wavering with refraction. She surfaces on the corner facing east, brightening as she comes up. *Twist:* the belt is a stream under the glass, so she goes under, and the slab looks deep for the first time.

24. **Reflection Leads.** Her reflection in the glass moves first: the dim copy under the surface slides to the corner and turns east, and she follows a moment later and settles exactly on top of it. *Twist:* the belt carries the reflection (it's the part of her down in the stream), and she simply catches up with herself.

25. **Mirror Swap.** A pane of glass rises at 45 degrees across the corner tile. She slides into it, and her reflection, already turned, is the one that steps out onto the corner facing east; then the pane sinks back into the board. *Twist:* a mirror is the classic way to turn light through 90 degrees, and the swap plays as a magic trick: did the real Maya just go into the glass?

26. **Photon.** On the express step she stretches into a streak of her own red light, a thin body with her eye as a bright head, and travels as a ray. It bends at the corner like light through a prism and condenses back into the Pebble facing east. *Twist:* in a world drawn with light, the fastest thing on the board briefly becomes light, and the corner bends her the way glass bends a ray.

27. **Lighthouse.** As the corner turns her, her eye becomes a soft beam that sweeps across the dark board from north to east, across empty glass and briefly over Jun, then dims as she settles. *Twist:* the quarter turn is drawn across the whole board, so someone glancing up from their phone still sees it, and Jun gets lit up as if caught.

28. **Revolving Door.** Four glass fins, the same bright fins as walls, rise from the corner tile as a revolving door. She glides into one quarter, the door turns 90 degrees, and she steps out facing east before the door sinks back into the glass. *Twist:* a revolving door is a machine for turning people exactly a quarter, built from Lumen's own wall material.

29. **Pneumatic Post.** A glass tube rises over the belt's path, bending through the corner like a pneumatic-post pipe. She shoots through it on a pulse of light, pops out on the corner facing east, and the tube melts back into the slab. *Twist:* express belt means express post, and the glass takes on a shape for a moment.

30. **Shape Sorter.** A Pebble-shaped shadow with a thin rim appears on the corner tile, already pointing east: it's made of Lumen's absence of light but is clearly not a pit. She slides along, rotates to match, and drops into it with a snug little settle as her light fills it. *Twist:* it's a baby's shape-sorter toy built from darkness; the room sees her destination and facing before she arrives, and enjoys the fit.

31. **The Hand of the Game.** A soft disc of warm light presses down on her from above like a fingertip, slides her two spaces the way a player slides a miniature, twists her a quarter turn at the corner and lifts away. *Twist:* this is how belts work in the cardboard game (someone reaches over and moves your robot), redone in Lumen's one warm light.

32. **Rules Diagram.** Before she moves, a dotted path of thin light draws itself on the glass ahead of her, with a curling arrow at the corner, like the illustration in a rulebook. She follows it exactly, and the dots go out as she passes over them. *Twist:* the rulebook comes to life; it suits the first belt ride of a game and can retire once everyone knows the rule.

33. **Just a Pawn.** The moment the belt takes her, she becomes a plain glass board-game pawn in her colour, with her pointer still showing her heading. She's slid along and turned, and pops back into her Pebble form on the corner facing east. *Twist:* when you're not in control you're just a pawn, a meta joke the room gets instantly.

34. **The Board Plays a Card.** Before the belts run, a card rises out of the belt, the same lit slide as the players' cards, reading "BELT x2" in thin type, and hovers by the corner. She's carried and turned, and the card dissolves. *Twist:* the board has a program too, and it plays its card the way a player does.

35. **Curling.** She's slid like a curling stone while two little brushes of light sweep the glass ahead of her. She turns slowly the whole way (the curl) and comes to rest dead centre on the corner, facing exactly east, as a target of rings flashes once. *Twist:* the turn isn't a snap at the corner but a curling stone's curl spread over the whole slide, and it lands on the button.

36. **Labyrinth Tilt.** The whole slab tips a few degrees east like a wooden marble-maze toy; she slides downhill, the slab tips again to steer her round into the corner, then levels, while Jun stays put on his space and wobbles indignantly. *Twist:* the board becomes a tilt toy; it's big, so keep it for a rare ride, and it ends with the board level and her exactly on the corner.

37. **Quarter Past.** As she reaches the corner, the twelve faint ticks of a clock face light around her ring. Her pointer is the hand, and it moves from twelve to three with one soft tick. *Twist:* north to east is twelve o'clock to three, so the turn becomes a time, quarter past, on a clock face anyone can read.

38. **Loyalty Card.** Each belt ride stamps a small dot of her colour into a row of five beside her name. This is ride five: the row fills and flashes, and thin type reads "5th ride free" before fading. Nothing in the game changes. *Twist:* the belt runs a loyalty scheme with no reward; across a game players watch their cards fill, and the empty prize is the joke.

39. **Belt Melody.** Each space she's carried leaves a short, thin column of light rising from the tile she left, its height a note: low for the express step, a fifth higher for the second. The corner adds a third column, the note that resolves the phrase, and the columns sink in order. *Twist:* every belt ride plays a tiny tune; different belt lengths make different tunes, and robots riding at the same time harmonise.

40. **Line Dance.** Thin type calls the steps like a line-dance caller, "step... step... quarter turn right!", and she does them on the beat with a little dip on each, while Jun bobs along on his own space. *Twist:* two steps and a quarter turn really is a line-dance phrase, and on a busy board every robot on a belt dances in unison.

41. **Strobe.** The room dims for a heartbeat and a warm-white strobe freezes her five times along the ride. Each flash leaves a faint glass copy of her, so her path ends as a fan of ghost Pebbles, each turned a little further, which fade in order as she settles facing east. *Twist:* Edgerton's stroboscope photographs happen live, and the turn reads as a sequence, the way the rulebook would draw it.

42. **The World Moves.** The camera locks onto her so she stays still on screen while the whole board, Jun included, slides west under her; at the corner the board rotates round her instead of her turning. Then the camera eases home and shows her on the corner facing east. *Twist:* from her point of view she never moved, the world did; keep it short, gentle and rare.

43. **Robot Cam.** A small inset window opens at the edge of the screen showing what her eye sees: the dark room to the north, sliding sideways. The turn swings the view round onto Jun, staring straight back, and the window closes. *Twist:* her point of view reveals the joke: she's been staring at nothing, and the belt turns her to face her rival.

44. **Under the River.** On the first belt ride of a game, the camera dips below the edge of the slab and looks up through the smoked glass. The stream of light runs overhead and her dark silhouette rides it, like a boat seen by a fish, turning at the bend, before the camera rises home. *Twist:* it's the only time the room ever sees the board from beneath, and one look explains "streams under the glass".

45. **Ride Photo.** At the corner a soft camera flash goes off, and a small framed snapshot of her mid-ride (facing the wrong way, eye wide) appears beside her name with a thin-type caption, then fades. *Twist:* it's the theme-park on-ride photo, of a robot who never asked to be on the ride.

46. **Mind the Corner.** As she nears the corner, thin type flows along in the stream under the glass, like a moving-walkway announcement: "the walkway is turning · please mind your heading". She's turned, and the words flow on down the south leg. *Twist:* the belt talks in an airport voice, and even its words obey the belt.

47. **Overshoot and Rewind.** The belt is overeager and carries her past the corner, halfway down the south leg. Then the board catches itself and rewinds: her trail winds back up like tape, she's pulled back onto the corner, and the turn to east plays properly. *Twist:* the rewind is drawn in Lumen's own trail, it ends on the exact rule, and it hints at where the belt goes next.

48. **Loop-the-Loop.** The stream peels off the glass as a ribbon of light and rises into a vertical loop in the dark above the belt. She rides it, upside down for a moment with her eye drawing a circle in the dark, and comes down onto the corner facing east as the ribbon lays itself back into the belt. *Twist:* the belt becomes a roller-coaster track drawn in light; she leaves the board but never touches the dark, so the gasp turns into a laugh (a rare, big variant).

49. **Express Delivery.** A ribbon of blue light lifts out of the belt as she sets off, wraps her like a parcel and ties itself in a bow. At the corner the bow tugs her round to east, and the ribbon unwraps and slides back into the stream. *Twist:* "express" is taken literally, and the belt itself is the wrapping.

50. **Belt Memory.** Every robot that rides a belt leaves a faint thread of its colour woven into the stream, so late in a game the busy belts are braided ribbons of everyone's colours; this ride adds Maya's red. *Twist:* the board quietly keeps the story of the game; nobody is told, and someone notices it in round four.

## The five to animate

1. **2. Wait, There's More** (the laugh): she relaxes after the express step and gets yanked mid-sigh, so the room learns "blue means two" as a running gag. It's all posture and eye light, and it fits the two real beats, so it can play every ride.
2. **30. Shape Sorter** (the satisfying one): a slot in her shape, made of darkness, waits on the corner, already pointing east, and she drops snugly into it. It forecasts the result, reads as one big shape, and doesn't wear out, so it's the everyday default.
3. **25. Mirror Swap** (the magic trick): a glass pane on the corner's diagonal turns her the way a mirror turns light, and her reflection steps out. It's a quick "wait, how?" made only of glass and reflection, and it works on any corner.
4. **47. Overshoot and Rewind** (the fake-out): the belt carries her too far, then the board rewinds her trail like tape and plays the turn properly. It's a deliberate rule-break that ends on the exact rule, uses big motion that reads at any size, and stays rare.
5. **22. The Koi** (the wonder): a creature of light lives in the belts and, once in a while, carries her round the bend with a flick of its tail. It's big under the glass, and its rarity gives the room a reason to watch every belt ride.
